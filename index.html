A project with the difficult work already behind it

This 1963 Land Rover 88 Series IIa was taken apart for restoration about thirty years ago. Since then it has passed through three owners, each moving it closer to the finish line. I've had a change of life and it's time to pass it on to someone who will complete it.

Nearly all of the steel has been regalvanized or powder coated. The chassis was powder coated and reassembled with all new parts, including new swivel balls, and the engine and a stainless steel exhaust are mounted on it. The aluminum body panels have been stripped of paint and are straight.

It comes with a Fairey overdrive, Fairey locking front hubs, a Safari top, and a large collection of new, new old stock, and spare parts, much of it bagged and labeled.

If you're looking for a Series Land Rover project, this one lets you skip straight to the rewarding part.
